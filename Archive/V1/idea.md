Purpose
To learn about coin math

Context
Athena is doing challenging math. I like visualizing things. I think this could be a fun quick distraction while working through them. The class of question is to use combinations of USD coins to make some amount. Ex. 50 coins to make 1 dollar.

I'm thinking of explaning this multiple ways.
1) figuratively, an app that allows you to simplify the question, permutations of the question, and displays actual coins. It will be a table top of sorts where the child can click and drag coins around.
2) diagrammatically, maybe now just a spreadsheet table like approach
3) symbolically, xP+yN+zD+aQ+bH+cDollars = amount. x+y+z+a+b+c=# of coins
4) Graphically, map these onto a graph and show solution sets.

In addition, I imagine that there are additional classes of math questions with coins. Maybe they will go into different tabs (coin 2, coin 3...)
